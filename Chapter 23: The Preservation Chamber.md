# Chapter 23: The Preservation Chamber

The final words written inside the *Kadalkol* remained fresh in everyone's mind as they stood inside the Chamber of Two Hearts.

*"Beyond the Chamber of Two Hearts lies the Path of Origins. There the true history remains untouched."*

For several moments, nobody spoke. The underground chamber that had revealed so many secrets now felt strangely quiet. The story of Athiyan and Mathi had changed the way they looked at their entire journey. What had begun as an adventure in search of a lost land had slowly become something much deeper. Every clue, every map, every artifact seemed to point toward a forgotten civilization that had fought to preserve its memory against time itself.

Joseph was the first to break the silence.

"I think Athiyan wanted someone to find this place one day," he said quietly.

Abinaya carefully closed the *Kadalkol* and secured it inside her bag. "Not just Athiyan," she replied. "Mathi too. Everything we have found so far tells us they planned this together."

Harish nodded in agreement. Looking around the chamber, he felt as though the ancient king and queen were still guiding them, leaving behind pieces of a trail for future generations to follow.

After checking their equipment and gathering their belongings, the five friends stepped into the passage beyond the chamber. The tunnel ahead was unlike any they had crossed before. There were no puzzles carved into the walls. No hidden mechanisms. No warning symbols. Instead, the path felt calm and purposeful, as though it had been built to guide visitors directly to something important.

As they walked, Abinaya noticed carvings appearing along the walls. They were not images of kings, armies, or battles. Instead, they showed ordinary people. Fishermen pulling in their nets. Children studying under teachers. Farmers working fertile land. Families gathered together. The carvings seemed to celebrate the lives of common people rather than rulers.

"Look at this," Abinaya said, stopping beside one of the stone panels.

The others gathered around her. Beneath the carving was an inscription written in ancient Tamil.

After carefully reading it, Abinaya smiled.

"It says, 'Knowledge survives when people choose to share it.'"

For a moment, everyone stood silently. The message was simple, yet powerful. It felt like a lesson carried across centuries.

The tunnel continued for several more minutes before finally opening into a pair of enormous stone doors. Both doors carried Athiyan's royal emblem. Time had worn away some of the details, but the symbol was still unmistakable.

Together, the friends pushed against the ancient doors. At first nothing happened. Then slowly, with a deep grinding sound, the doors began to move. Dust drifted through the air as the massive stone slabs opened inward.

The sight waiting beyond them left everyone speechless.

The chamber on the other side was enormous.

Unlike the underground rooms they had previously explored, this place felt carefully organized. Stone cabinets stood neatly arranged around the room. Protective stone cases lined the walls. Every object seemed intentionally preserved.

Keerthi was the first to approach one of the stone cases. Inside were five large scroll tubes carved from polished stone. Each contained a map, preserved so carefully that it looked as though it had been sealed only yesterday.

Nearby, Harish discovered three thick books resting inside another compartment. Their covers were decorated with ancient Tamil inscriptions and symbols unlike anything they had seen before.

Meanwhile, Joseph called everyone toward the center of the chamber.

When they reached him, they immediately understood his excitement.

Before him sat a stone chest.

Inside the chest were dozens of ancient gold coins.

Each coin carried Athiyan's royal seal along with intricate carvings of sea waves and ancient Tamil symbols. The craftsmanship was extraordinary. Even after surviving for centuries, the coins still gleamed faintly in the lantern light.

Sharan carefully picked one up and examined it.

"This is incredible," he whispered.

Joseph smiled. "The coin we found near the wall wasn't a coincidence after all."

Abinaya turned one of the coins over in her hand.

"This isn't just treasure," she said.

"It's proof."

Everyone understood what she meant. These coins were evidence. Evidence that Athiyan had been real. Evidence that his kingdom had existed. Evidence that the stories passed down through generations contained far more truth than anyone had imagined.

For nearly two hours the group carefully explored every corner of the chamber. They documented everything they could, photographed the inscriptions, and compared symbols with the records they already possessed. In the end, they decided to take only what they could reasonably carry—five maps, three books, copies of the stone tablet inscriptions, and a small bag of the royal coins.

The rest they left untouched.

Somehow, it felt wrong to disturb what Athiyan and Mathi had protected for so long.

As they prepared to leave, Harish suddenly felt a cool breeze brush against his face.

At first he thought he had imagined it.

Then he felt it again.

"Wait," he said.

The others stopped.

Harish followed the faint airflow toward the far side of the chamber. Hidden behind a section of stonework was another narrow passage. Unlike the tunnels they had traveled before, this one sloped upward.

Toward the surface.

Excitement immediately spread through the group.

Without wasting time, they entered the passage and began climbing. The higher they went, the brighter the darkness became. Soon a faint glow appeared ahead.

Sunlight.

Real sunlight.

After spending so long beneath the island, the sight felt almost unreal.

The group quickened their pace. Step after step, they climbed until they finally reached the end of the passage. Joseph pushed open the final stone doorway.

Warm sunlight flooded inside.

Fresh sea air rushed over them.

For a few moments, nobody said anything. They simply stood there, enjoying the feeling of being back beneath the open sky.

Then they stepped outside.

The ocean stretched endlessly before them. Waves crashed against the rocks below while seabirds circled overhead. After everything they had endured underground, the outside world felt more beautiful than ever.

Joseph immediately pulled out his compass while Sharan compared their position with his notes. Keerthi studied the coastline, and Abinaya unfolded one of the ancient maps.

Several minutes later, Harish suddenly looked up.

"We're on the opposite side of the island."

Everyone froze.

The compass confirmed it.

The underground city stretched beneath the entire island.

Then Abinaya noticed something else.

Her eyes widened as she examined one of the newly recovered maps.

"This island isn't marked as a small island," she said quietly.

The others quickly gathered around her.

A name was written across the map.

A forgotten name connected directly to Athiyan's dynasty.

And according to the ancient records, the land beneath their feet had once been one of the most important centers of Athiyan's coastal kingdom.

The five friends stared at the map in stunned silence.

Their journey had just revealed another secret.

And somewhere within the maps they now carried, an even greater mystery was waiting to be uncovered.
