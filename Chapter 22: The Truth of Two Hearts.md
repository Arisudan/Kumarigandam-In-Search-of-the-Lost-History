Chapter 22
The Truth of Two Hearts

The chamber remained silent after the massive stone door had opened. The words from the *Kadalkol* book continued to echo in everyone's minds. Joseph still held the ancient key in his hand while the others carefully examined the newly revealed chamber. There had to be a reason why the key had been hidden inside the wall mechanism. Nothing they had discovered so far had been accidental.

Harish slowly looked around the chamber. The walls were covered with carvings that had survived centuries beneath the earth and sea. Sharan carefully observed every symbol while Keerthi unfolded the map once again and compared it with the routes they had already traveled. Abinaya stood quietly with the *Kadalkol* book in her hands, reading through the pages once more.

As she carefully turned another page, something caught her attention.

"Wait," she said softly.

Everyone immediately gathered around her.

Abinaya pointed toward a faint drawing near the edge of the page. It showed a circular structure surrounded by four smaller symbols. At first it looked insignificant, but after everything they had discovered, they knew that even the smallest detail could hold an important clue.

"Look at this," she said.

Sharan studied the drawing closely.

"It looks like some kind of mechanism."

Harish nodded. "Or a lock."

Joseph raised the lantern higher and looked deeper into the chamber. At the far end of the room, partially hidden in darkness, stood a circular stone platform. They had overlooked it before because of the shadows.

The group carefully walked toward it.

As they got closer, the design became clearer. The platform contained four separate slots around its edges. At the center was a large keyhole carved into the stone.

For a moment nobody spoke.

Then Joseph smiled.

"This is what we've been searching for."

Each artifact they had collected suddenly made sense.

Harish stepped forward first. He carefully placed the ancient Chola coin into the first slot. A faint glow appeared beneath the stone.

Sharan then placed the Maragatham gemstone into the second slot. Soft green light spread through the carvings surrounding the platform.

Keerthi carefully placed the mysterious orange gemstone into the third slot. The chamber immediately filled with a warm golden-orange glow unlike anything they had seen before.

Now only one object remained.

Joseph looked down at the key resting in his hand. He took a deep breath before inserting it into the central keyhole.

The moment he turned it, the entire chamber shook.

A deep sound echoed through the underground structure. Dust fell from the ceiling as ancient mechanisms awakened after centuries of silence. Streams of green, gold, and orange light flowed from the three artifacts toward the center of the platform and merged around the key.

Then the platform slowly began to move.

Stone slid against stone.

A hidden compartment emerged from beneath the floor.

Everyone watched in complete silence.

When the movement stopped, they stepped closer.

Inside the compartment were two beautifully preserved stone tablets.

There was no gold.

No treasure chest.

No pile of jewels.

Only the two tablets.

Yet somehow, all of them felt that what lay before them was far more valuable than treasure.

Abinaya carefully knelt beside the tablets and began reading the ancient Tamil inscriptions carved into them.

The first tablet spoke about King Athiyan.

It described him as a ruler, a protector, and a visionary leader who governed the Seven Eastern Coastal Territories of Kumarigandam. The inscriptions praised his wisdom and his dedication to his people.

The second tablet spoke about Mathi.

As Abinaya continued reading, her expression slowly changed.

"This is surprising," she whispered.

"What is it?" Harish asked.

Abinaya looked up.

"It says Mathi was not only Athiyan's queen."

Everyone stared at her.

She continued reading.

"She was the Keeper of Knowledge."

The chamber fell silent.

The inscription described Mathi as the guardian of the kingdom's wisdom, records, maps, and secrets. While Athiyan protected the people and lands, Mathi protected the knowledge that defined their civilization. Together they had built a system to preserve their history even if the kingdom itself disappeared.

The discovery changed everything.

For all this time, the group had believed they were searching for a lost kingdom hidden beneath the ocean.

Now they realized they were searching for something far greater.

A legacy.

Abinaya continued reading the final section of the inscription.

Her voice became softer as she spoke the words.

*"The seas may rise. The lands may fall. Kingdoms may disappear beneath the waves. Yet truth shall remain. Not in gold. Not in power. Not in riches. But in the bonds we leave behind."*

Nobody spoke.

The words felt strangely alive.

As if Athiyan himself had written them for the future.

The next lines revealed another truth.

*"Those who seek wealth shall lose their way. Those who seek knowledge shall find the path. Kumarigandam is not lost. It waits for those who understand its purpose."*

Joseph slowly sat down on a nearby stone.

"So all of this wasn't protecting treasure."

Harish nodded.

"It was protecting history."

"And knowledge," Abinaya added.

Keerthi looked at the stone tablets.

"And memories."

For a brief moment, Harish glanced toward Abinaya. She was still reading the inscriptions carefully. The story of Athiyan and Mathi suddenly felt much closer than it had before.

Athiyan had trusted Mathi with the future of their civilization.

Mathi had protected his legacy long after his kingdom disappeared.

Their bond had survived centuries.

The realization quietly stayed in Harish's mind.

Nearby, Sharan stood beside Keerthi, studying the carvings. Even he could not stop thinking about how powerful such trust must have been.

Joseph finally broke the silence.

"This changes everything."

Everyone agreed.

The journey was no longer about discovering a lost island.

It was no longer about treasure.

It was about uncovering the truth of an entire civilization.

As Abinaya carefully closed the *Kadalkol* book, she noticed one final line written near the bottom of the page.

Her eyes widened.

"What is it?" Harish asked.

Abinaya slowly looked up.

"The book says these tablets are only the beginning."

Silence returned.

Everyone waited.

She continued reading.

*"Beyond the Chamber of Two Hearts lies the Path of Origins. There the true history remains untouched."*

The group looked toward the darkness beyond the chamber.

Their adventure was far from over.

In fact, it felt as though it had only just begun.
